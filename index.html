SensorPush Radio Setup

How to use it

  1. Put the new battery in the sensor first. Stand next to it (closer than 30 ft).
  2. Press Connect a sensor. The list shows only names that start with the text in the Sensor name starts with box. To make the list shorter, type more of the name, exactly as nRF Connect shows it.
  3. Press Blink LED: the sensor in your hand flashes. If another one flashes, disconnect and pick the next name.
  4. Press Set to +5 dBm. The page writes it, reads it back and shows Confirmed.
  5. Press Disconnect and go to the next sensor. At the end, press Copy results and paste them in the Heat Safety Monitor (Battery tab).
First sensor only: check that the setting stays. After Confirmed, take the battery out for 10 seconds, put it back, connect again and look at "TX power now". If it went back to the old value, tell the HSE team before doing more sensors.

SensorPush: more power = more range, at the cost of battery life; they suggest +5 dBm for signal problems and say the battery still lasts over two years in typical use.
